Dear Emma,

In Camden everyone is almost famous. *Soft Power* is a verse drama in
three acts and ten scenes, set across Maundy Thursday, Good Friday and
Holy Saturday in the Camden music scene. It’s a loop — the first line is
the last, and the cast the final scene seems to kill is the cast the
first scene is mourning.

The protagonist is Jolyon Dox, an A&R man at his younger brother Cosmo’s
wake in a Camden basement called Sunset. By Holy Saturday he’s worked
out Cosmo never died, the funeral was theatre, and the gig his new band
are about to play will be the cast’s last visible night. Modelled on
Dante and Eliot; the form follows Evaristo’s *The Emperor’s Babe*, the
closer contemporaries are Caleb Femi’s *Poor* (Penguin, 2020) and Kae
Tempest’s *Brand New Ancients* (Picador, 2013). About 80 pages typeset,
complete in V2 draft.

I was born and raised in Camden and went to Highgate, where Eliot taught
Betjeman in 1916 — the lineage the book opens on (the epigraph is
Betjeman). I DJed and wrote songs through my teens and twenties on the
London indie circuit. *Soft Power* was first staged at The Nines,
Brixton, in 2017, with Slix Fleeingham of Ruff Sqwad playing Joly. I
live in Prague.

I’m submitting to a small number of agents at once. Attached: a twenty-
page typeset sample (opening pages and the closing scene) and a short
synopsis. Full manuscript ready when you want it.

Yours,
