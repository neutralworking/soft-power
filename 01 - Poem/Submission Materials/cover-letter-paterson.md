Dear Emma,

I am writing with a verse drama, *Soft Power*, which I think may be of
interest given your work with Bernardine Evaristo.

The poem is set in the Camden music scene across three Easter days. Its
subject is the attention economy — what it costs to make art honestly
inside it, and what people do when they decide they would rather
disappear than compromise. The form follows Evaristo's *Emperor's Babe*;
the closer contemporaries are Femi's *Poor* (Penguin, 2020) and
Tempest's *Brand New Ancients* (Picador, 2013). The manuscript ends at
the moment it begins, and runs to approximately eighty pages.

I grew up in Camden and spent much of my twenties writing and playing
music there. *Soft Power* was staged at The Nines, Brixton, in 2017.

I am enclosing a twenty-page sample and a synopsis, and am submitting to
a small number of agents simultaneously. The full manuscript is available
at your request.

Yours sincerely,
Luke
