Dear Emma,

*Soft Power* is a verse drama in three acts and ten scenes, set across
Maundy Thursday, Good Friday and Holy Saturday in the Camden music scene.
Jolyon Dox is attending his younger brother Cosmo’s wake at a club called
Sunset; by the third act, the brothers are in the same room for the first
time since Cosmo faked his own death at the Port of Spezia. The poem is a
loop — its first line is its last, and the cast the final scene appears to
kill is the cast the opening scene is mourning. About 80 pages typeset,
complete in V2 draft. In form it follows Evaristo’s *The Emperor’s Babe*;
the closer contemporaries are Caleb Femi’s *Poor* (Penguin, 2020) and
Kae Tempest’s *Brand New Ancients* (Picador, 2013).

I was born and raised in Camden and went to Highgate, where Eliot taught
Betjeman in 1916. I DJed and wrote songs through my teens and twenties on
the London indie circuit. *Soft Power* was staged at The Nines, Brixton,
in 2017, with Slix Fleeingham of Ruff Sqwad playing Joly. I live in Prague.

I’m submitting to a small number of agents at once. Attached: a twenty-
page typeset sample and a short synopsis. Full manuscript ready when you
want it.
